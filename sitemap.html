Help

This help page explains the full VanaNidhi navigation story, grouped by office role and workflow. Use it as a client-facing guide to understand how each module fits into the Budget & Finance Management System.

1

Login

User enters through HQ, Circle, Division or Range role.

2

APO

Lower offices prepare APO; HQ sees approved APO as Budget Estimate.

3

Allocation

HQ releases allocation/allotment to offices and heads.

4

Tracking

Progress, wage requests, surrender and revenue are tracked.

5

Reports

HQ consolidates reports, annexures and financial controls.

1. Login & Role Entry

Start at login and choose the correct office role.

Default LoginLanding login screen for all roles.HQ LoginHeadquarters/state-level access.Range LoginRange office role entry.Division LoginDivision office role entry.Circle LoginCircle office role entry.

2. HQ APO Decision Flow

HQ receives submissions, approves/rejects, and converts approved APOs into Budget Estimate.

APO Submission InboxView, approve, reject or return APO submissions.Budget Estimate - HQHQ-level Budget Estimate page.APO Period ManagementControl submission/approval date windows.Approved APOsApproved register.Rejected / Returned APOsReturned/rejected register.

3. HQ Budget Allocation & Finance

After approval, HQ controls allocation, allotment register, resumption and revenue.

Budget HeadsBudget head master and formats.Budget AllocationAllocate/release funds.Allotment RegisterTrack released funds, expenditure and balance.Direct ResumptionHQ pulls back available balance directly.Resumption RequestsRegister of pull-back actions.Funds & Pending BillsPending bills and fund tracking.Revenue / eTreasuryRevenue target and treasury view.

3A. HQ Estimator

Estimator is the cost-estimation workspace for forest works. It supports multiple selections such as office, budget head, work category, unit, quantity, terrain difficulty, transport loading, labour/material split and contingency.

Forest Work EstimatorBuild a work estimate using selections, rate library values and adjustments. Rate LibraryReference library for standard forest work rates and units. Estimate RegisterTrack draft, verified and returned estimates.

Division has its own APO while also reviewing and forwarding Range submissions.
