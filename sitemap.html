Story of the System

1

Prepare before the budget

The department begins by setting offices, officers, budget heads, components and schedules. Range and Division offices prepare APO and budget estimate data. Performance Budget formats convert the plan into physical and financial targets.

MastersAPOBudget OverviewPerformance Budget
2

Review through office hierarchy

Data submitted from the field is checked at higher levels. Division reviews Range submissions, Circle reviews Division submissions, and HQ reviews Circle submissions. Each level can forward, return, correct, or approve.

RangeDivisionCircleHQ
3

Allocate the approved budget

Once provision is available, the system uses Budget Head masters and role-wise screens to distribute allocation, capture additional fund requirement, surrender/resumption and revenue targets.

Budget HeadsFunds & BillsRevenue
4

Track work after allocation

After budget allocation, implementation begins. Forestry estimates, field progress, monthly financial/physical achievement, pending bills and wage liabilities are monitored from one system.

Forestry WorksProgressPending Bills
5

Generate reports for review

Reports and annexures provide the final review layer. Officers can see head-wise expenditure, performance budget outputs, revenue reports, progress summaries and client-ready annexure formats.
