VanaNidhi Workflow Sitemap

The portal is organised as a story: first prepare APO, then approve and convert it into Budget Estimate at HQ, then allocate funds and track execution.

1. Pre-Budget Activities

Range, Division and Circle users work with APO - Annual Plan Operations. They prepare Plan/Non-Plan entries, request Prior AS where required and submit Action Plans.

Prior ASAction Plan

2. Approval Pipeline

APO moves through Division and Circle review. Users can filter proposed, approved and returned items and take view/action decisions.

Division APO SubmissionsCircle APO Submissions

3. HQ Budget Estimate

At HQ, approved APO becomes Budget Estimate. HQ also controls APO submission and approval date periods.

HQ Budget EstimateAPO Period

Budget Allocation

After approval, allocation details are published and head-wise fund utilisation begins.

Range AllocationFunds & Bills

Post-Budget Tracking

Offices update physical progress, financial progress, wages and revenue target achievements.

Progress UpdationWage Excel UploadRevenue Target

Reports & Review

HQ and offices use reports to review APO, Budget Estimate, progress, revenue and pending liabilities.
