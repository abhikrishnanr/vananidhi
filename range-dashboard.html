Range Office Dashboard

A compact working console for the Range Office: prepare APO entries, view approved APOs, track allotment and expenditure, update progress, request wage funds, surrender unutilized balance and monitor revenue target.

Create APO Entry View Allotment Surrender Entry Wage Fund Request

What this office can do

  • Submit APO data and supporting documents
  • View approved APOs and allotment details
  • Update physical/financial progress
  • Submit wage fund and surrender requests

Responsibility Note

  • PO Components and Subcomponents are handled by HQ
  • Range can submit/correct APOs, not approve them
  • Forwarding starts from Division level onwards
